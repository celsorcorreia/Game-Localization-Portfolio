## Controlled distinctions

- **chaos / chaotic:** generic English concepts.
- **Khaoz / Khaotic:** setting-specific names and branded terminology.
- **Khoz:** Titan of Chaos.
- **khaosite:** unstable crystalline substance.
- **Goddess:** member of the Pantheon.
- **Entity:** divine or cosmic being outside the Pantheon.
- **Titan:** primordial being associated with fundamental or catastrophic forces.
- **Divinity:** gender-neutral collective term.
