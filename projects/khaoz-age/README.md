# Khaoz Age — Narrative Localization Case Study

**Khaoz Age** is an original fantasy RPG and worldbuilding setting featuring divine cosmology, titans, cultures, magic, anomalies, and extensive lore.

## Localization focus

- lore and codex prose;
- names, titles, and epithets;
- cultures and factions;
- classes, conditions, and game terminology;
- consistency across a large fictional universe;
- preservation of mythic tone;
- documentation of invented words and naming systems.
