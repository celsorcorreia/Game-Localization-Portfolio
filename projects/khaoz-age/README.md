# Khaoz Age RPG — Narrative Localization Case Study

**Khaoz Age RPG** is an original dark fantasy tabletop role-playing setting centred on Sarkan, divine cosmology, Titans, cultures, khaosite, Khaotic Anomalies, and extensive interconnected lore.

## Localization focus

- lore and codex prose;
- coined terminology and branded spellings;
- names, titles, and epithets;
- divine taxonomy;
- cultures, lineages, and factions;
- historical events and geographic names;
- classes, conditions, and game terminology;
- consistency across a large fictional universe;
- preservation of mythic dark fantasy voice;
- documentation of naming and localization decisions.
