# Localization Style Guide

## Language variants

- Source/target pair: English ↔ Brazilian Portuguese
- Portuguese locale: `pt-BR`
- English locale: `en`

## General principles

- Prefer natural player-facing language over literal syntax.
- Use established genre terminology when it improves comprehension.
- Avoid unnecessary English borrowings in Portuguese unless they are conventional or part of the product identity.
- Preserve deliberate ambiguity only when it serves narrative or gameplay.
- Keep UI strings concise without sacrificing clarity.

## Brazilian Portuguese

- Use sentence case unless the interface requires another convention.
- Prefer direct verbs in commands and tutorial prompts.
- Avoid European Portuguese forms unless required by the project.
- Use inclusive and neutral constructions when they remain natural and readable.

## English

- Use clear international English unless the project specifies a regional variety.
- Avoid overly literal structures transferred from Portuguese.
- Preserve naming patterns consistently across factions, abilities, items, and locations.
